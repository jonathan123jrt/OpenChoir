OpenChoir for macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11+)

INSTALL
  1. Right-click install.command and choose Open. On newer macOS, if it's
     blocked, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  2. It installs:
       OpenChoir           - the AI choir instrument (VST3 + AU)
       OpenChoir Ensemble  - the real-time choir effect (VST3 + AU)
       the AI engine       - into ~/Library/Application Support/OpenChoir/ai
     Then it offers to run the one-time AI setup: about 6 GB, 10-30 minutes.
  3. Restart your DAW, or rescan plug-ins.

USE
  Put OpenChoir on an INSTRUMENT track and open it.
    1 Vocal - drop in a dry, single-voice vocal
    2 Choir - choose the choir, mic, octaves and harmonies, key, stereo/mono and
              quality, then click Create Vocals
    3 Mix   - set each layer's level and mute, the mic and Lo-Cut / Brightness /
              Reverb, then drag the choir (or one layer) onto a track

  Renders are saved in ~/Music/OpenChoir/Renders.
  OpenChoir.app is a standalone version that runs without a DAW.
